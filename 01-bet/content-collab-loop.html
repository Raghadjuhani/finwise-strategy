FinWise Co. · Product-Led Growth

Content & Collaboration Growth Loop

A shared financial report is the content surface: it carries the product outside FinWise, and the outside viewer's own need to interact with it is what pulls them back in as a new user.
product boundary
Inside FinWise
Trigger
User builds a report on real data
A cash-flow forecast or budget-vs-actual view, generated from the business's own imported data.
↓
Stage 1 · Create
Report becomes a shareable, branded link
FinWise turns the model into a live, FinWise-branded view rather than a static export.
↓
Stage 2 · Share
Link goes to an outside stakeholder
Sent to an investor, lender, or board member for review ahead of a decision or meeting.
Outside FinWise
Stage 3 · Encounter
Stakeholder opens the live report
They view real, current numbers in a FinWise-branded interface — not a screenshot or PDF.
↓
Stage 4 · Gate
Wants to comment or adjust an assumption
Interacting further — leaving a note, changing a variable, drilling into a line — requires an account.
↓
Outcome
Stakeholder starts their own reverse trial
They sign up to interact fully, re-entering FinWise as a new user who will build and share their own reports.
Trigger — enters the loop
Core stages — create → share → encounter → gate
Outcome — restarts the loop with a new user
Where Stage 1 shows up
Reflected by the Import Data % and Modeling % activation metrics — content can't be built until data is in.
Where the loop leaks
A viewer who never opens the shared link, or opens it but never hits the Gate, doesn't produce a new signup.
Compounding mechanism
Each report can be viewed by several stakeholders, so one Trigger can produce more than one Outcome.
