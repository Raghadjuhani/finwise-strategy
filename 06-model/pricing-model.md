# The Model · FinWise

> Module 6 · Pricing & Monetization. The pricing & packaging recommendation memo for FinWise.

## Stage + model

Stage 1 · Value Creation. With 2% trial conversion and 40% one-year retention, we haven’t yet proven at scale that users experience the value we’re built to deliver — any pricing move has to close that gap first, not capture revenue from a value story that isn’t proven yet.

Model: Reverse-trial → Subscription. Full access up front, converting to a recurring paid tier. Fits how FinWise delivers value: forecasts and models compound in usefulness as more real data accumulates, and the product is used repeatedly, not once. Freemium is premature at this stage — a permanent free tier would mask the value problem instead of forcing us to solve it.

_____

## Recommendation

Move the paywall from a fixed day-14 calendar cutoff to a value-triggered boundary: it appears the moment a trial user completes their first personalized financial model on real imported data — the point where they’ve actually experienced the product’s core value, reached faster through personalized onboarding (user-type quiz + fast result on real data) and reinforced by the habit loop (Trigger → Action → Reward → Investment) that keeps them returning before that moment arrives.

This is a packaging bet, not a price change — we’re not asking users to pay more, we’re asking them to decide at the right moment instead of an arbitrary one.

_____

## Model signal

If this is working, conversions should cluster tightly around the activation event (first completed personalized model) rather than scattering across, or getting lost before, day 14. Concretely:

	•	Trial→Paid conversion rate moves from 2% toward the 10% target, read at 4 weeks.
	•	Time-to-conversion should compress and concentrate near the activation moment, not spread evenly across the trial window.
	•	One-year retention holds within 3 points of the 40% baseline (guardrail) — confirming the lift comes from better-timed, better-proven value, not from converting users who churn faster
