# The Signals · FinWise

> Module 4 · Data & Analytics. The data pattern you found and the indicators you'll steer by.

## The data pattern

Sessions Per User shows a Ceiling pattern, climbing through 2024 then flattening at 9 by Sept–Oct, the same months with two of the highest conversion rates (2.08%, 2.04%). That co-movement (+0.58 correlation) is why it's the leading indicator, but the ceiling means conversion gains may need other levers once engagement caps out.

_____

## Leading indicators

Time-to-first-personalized-model (how fast a trial user reaches a result matched to their user type on their own real data) and habit-loop cycle completions (Trigger resolved → Action taken → Reward seen), since both predict conversion and retention before they show up in the lagging Trial→Paid or 1-year retention numbers.

_____

## Lagging indicators

Trial to Paid conversion rate, currently 2%, and 1 year retention rate, currently 40%. Both are business outcomes we can't move directly, we earn them by moving Average Sessions Per User consistently over time.
